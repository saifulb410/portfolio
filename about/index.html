About Saiful Islam

Saiful Islam (also written Md Saiful Islam) is a Business Development and Operations professional from Bangladesh. He is the founder of WeTrainEducation & Tech OPC and the Business Development Manager at WeMasterTrade LTD.

He is studying Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Global Village (UGV) in Barishal, and is currently in the 8th semester. That technical background shapes how he works: he is comfortable with payment systems, support tooling and the product side of a business, not only the commercial side.

What I do

My work sits where business growth meets day-to-day operations. At WeMasterTrade I develop the Bangladesh market: I acquire affiliates, partners and influencers, grow the customer community, coordinate local marketing, and manage the team that supports customers. I also work on payment gateway and payment operations, bring product feedback back to the product team, and own reporting and KPIs.

As founder of WeTrainEducation & Tech, I lead a company that offers professional training courses in web development, marketing, design and business management, plus software and marketing services such as CRM, WhatsApp Business and SMS platforms for small and medium businesses.
